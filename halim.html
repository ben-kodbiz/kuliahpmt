person About Ustaz Halim Hassan

Ustaz Halim Hassan is a respected Islamic scholar who specializes in traditional Islamic medicine and Malay cultural practices from an Islamic perspective. With extensive knowledge of both classical Islamic texts and traditional Malay customs, he bridges the gap between historical practices and contemporary Islamic guidance.

Areas of Expertise

  • Traditional Islamic Medicine
  • Malay Cultural Practices
  • Islamic Perspective on Customs
  • Herbal Remedies in Islam
  • Historical Islamic Practices

Teaching Methodology

Ustaz Halim takes a holistic approach to Islamic education, combining classical scholarship with cultural sensitivity. His methodology includes:

  • Historical analysis of traditional practices
  • Islamic legal rulings on cultural customs
  • Integration of prophetic medicine (Tibb al-Nabawi)
  • Practical applications for contemporary life
  • Cultural preservation within Islamic boundaries

Notable Contributions

  • "Perubatan Tradisional & Adat Resam Orang Melayu" Series
  • Research on Islamic perspectives of Malay customs
  • Educational programs on prophetic medicine
  • Lectures on cultural preservation in Islam
  • Publications on traditional Islamic remedies

Philosophy

Ustaz Halim believes in preserving beneficial cultural practices while ensuring they remain within Islamic boundaries. He emphasizes the importance of understanding the wisdom behind traditional practices and applying them appropriately in modern times while maintaining strict adherence to Islamic principles.
