person About Ustaz Adli Mohd Saad

Ustaz Adli Mohd Saad is a distinguished Islamic scholar specializing in Islamic creed (Aqidah) and methodology (Manhaj). With extensive knowledge of classical Islamic texts and contemporary challenges, he provides clear and practical guidance on Islamic beliefs and their application in modern life.

Areas of Expertise

  • Islamic Creed (Aqidah)
  • Methodology of the Salaf (Manhaj Salaf)
  • Principles of Quranic Exegesis (Usul Tafsir)
  • Hadith Terminology (Mustolah al-Hadith)
  • Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh)

Teaching Methodology

Ustaz Adli employs a systematic and scholarly approach to Islamic education. His methodology includes:

  • Rooted in classical Islamic texts and scholarly consensus
  • Clear explanations of complex theological concepts
  • Practical application of Islamic principles
  • Balanced approach between tradition and contemporary relevance
  • Emphasis on understanding the wisdom behind Islamic rulings

Notable Contributions

  • "Aqidah Islam Manhaj Salaf" Comprehensive Series
  • "Usul Tafsir" Extensive Commentary Series
  • "Al-I'tisham" Commentary by Imam Asy-Syatibi
  • Lectures on the Four Schools of Islamic Jurisprudence
  • Educational programs on hadith methodology

Philosophy

Ustaz Adli believes in returning to the pure methodology of the Salaf (early generations of Muslims) while remaining relevant to contemporary challenges. He emphasizes the importance of sound creed as the foundation of a Muslim's faith and practice, advocating for a balanced approach that combines scholarly rigor with practical applicability.
